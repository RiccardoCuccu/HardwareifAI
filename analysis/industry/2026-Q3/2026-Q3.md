# Industry Analysis Snapshot: 2026-Q3

- **Date/quarter**: 2026-Q3
- **Companies scanned**: 7 companies with selected postings this quarter: Cerebras, Etched, Google, Meta, NVIDIA, OpenAI Hardware, Tenstorrent. 14 postings were selected in total: Cerebras (2), Etched (1), Google (5), Meta (1), NVIDIA (1), OpenAI Hardware (3), Tenstorrent (1).
- **Skills cluster observed**: ranked by how many of the 14 selected postings mention the term (count = number of postings, not raw keyword hits): Verilog (13 of 14), SystemVerilog (12 of 14), RTL design (10 of 14), Python (10 of 14), microarchitecture (10 of 14), verification (12 of 14), ASIC design/architecture (8 of 14), timing (7 of 14), synthesis (6 of 14), CDC (5 of 14), RDC (5 of 14), digital design (5 of 14), Perl (5 of 14), post-silicon bring-up (5 of 14). At moderate frequency: interconnect design (4 of 14), design-for-test/DFT (4 of 14), Tcl (4 of 14), power optimization (3 of 14), formal verification (3 of 14), PCIe (3 of 14), Ethernet (3 of 14). At lower frequency: power analysis (2 of 14), UVM (2 of 14), C/C++ (2 of 14), memory hierarchy (2 of 14), emulation (2 of 14), coherency/cache-coherent (2 of 14), AXI (1 of 14), NoC/network-on-chip (1 of 14), VLSI (1 of 14). All figures above are grounded in the "Listed required skills/technologies" and body text of the 14 selected posting records, not extrapolated.
- **Notes**: Every selected posting this quarter centers on RTL/ASIC design or design verification for an AI accelerator, TPU, or AI-native SoC component; five of the seven companies (Cerebras, Etched, Meta, NVIDIA, OpenAI Hardware) are directly AI-accelerator-focused employers, while Google's five selected postings are all scoped to TPU-specific teams rather than general silicon roles, consistent with the stricter relevance rubric this scan applies (both hands-on RTL/chip-design discipline and AI-accelerator-specific scope required). Seniority distribution across the 14 postings: 7 senior (Cerebras x2, Etched, Google Senior Design and Integration Engineer, Google Senior TPU RTL Design Engineer Networking, Meta, OpenAI SOC Architect), 4 mid (Google RTL Design Engineer TPU and ML, Google Silicon RTL Design Engineer PhD, Google TPU Hardware Design Engineer, OpenAI Design Verification Engineer), 1 entry (NVIDIA, a PhD new-college-grad research role with no stated minimum-years figure), and 2 postings spanning two tiers (OpenAI Senior RTL Engineer Interconnect Design, listed senior/staff with no stated years; Tenstorrent Sr. Engineer RTL Design AI Hardware, listed mid/senior, explicitly assessed at interview time rather than fixed to a posted level). Where a minimum-years figure was stated, it ranged from 2+ years (Google Silicon RTL Design Engineer PhD, post-PhD) up to 10+ years (Meta Design Verification Engineer); four postings (NVIDIA, OpenAI Senior RTL Engineer, OpenAI SOC Architect, Tenstorrent) stated no concrete years figure and are recorded with tier only, per the standing rule against inventing a number the posting itself does not state. Two Cerebras postings for the same role (Senior Front End Design Engineer, Microarchitecture) were both selected, one for the Sunnyvale HQ location and one for a Bangalore, India location, each treated as a distinct posting record. Compensation was stated on 8 of the 14 postings (Cerebras Sunnyvale, Etched, four of five Google postings, Meta, NVIDIA, OpenAI's three postings share one shared range), spanning roughly $138K to $445K base depending on level and location; onsite/hybrid split roughly evenly, with two postings (Tenstorrent, one OpenAI posting) explicitly hybrid.
