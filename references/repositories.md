## Extra

**[openai-triton]** OpenAI (n.d.). *Triton: a language and compiler for custom deep learning kernels*. https://github.com/openai/triton  
Shows how high-level neural operations map to hardware memory hierarchies and block-level parallelism, useful software-awareness context for hardware engineers even though it isn't silicon design.

**[google-research-circuit-training]** Google Research (n.d.). *Circuit Training: deep reinforcement learning for chip floorplanning*. https://github.com/google-research/circuit_training  
Used across multiple generations of Google TPU design, it shows concretely how AI methods optimise macro placement, wirelength, timing, and routability in real accelerator silicon.

**[microsoft-ai-for-beginners]** Microsoft (n.d.). *AI For Beginners: a 12-week open-source curriculum*. https://github.com/microsoft/AI-For-Beginners  
Software-focused, but its from-scratch NumPy implementations of MLPs, autograd, convolutions, and attention are useful golden-reference models for RTL verification testbenches.
