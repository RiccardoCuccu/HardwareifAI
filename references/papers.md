**[devlin2018]** Devlin, J., Chang, M. W., Lee, K. and Toutanova, K. (2018). *BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805  
Introduces deep bidirectional pretraining, the NLP fine-tuning standard until GPT-style few-shot approaches took over.

**[goodfellow2014]** Goodfellow, I. et al. (2014). *Generative Adversarial Networks*. https://doi.org/10.1145/3422622  
Introduces the adversarial paradigm for generation, the direct progenitor of most image and video generative AI before diffusion models.

**[ho2020]** Ho, J., Jain, A. and Abbeel, P. (2020). *Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239  
The foundational paper behind modern diffusion-based image and video generation.

**[jumper2021]** Jumper, J. et al. (2021). *Highly Accurate Protein Structure Prediction with AlphaFold*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2  
The highest-profile AI-applied-to-science result, solving the decades-old protein folding problem.

**[kaplan2020]** Kaplan, J. et al. (2020). *Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361  
The paper that economically justified pouring ever more hardware investment into ever-larger models, directly shaping the compute buildout that followed.

**[lindholm2008]** Lindholm, E. et al. (2008). *NVIDIA Tesla: A Unified Graphics and Computing Architecture*. https://doi.org/10.1109/MM.2008.31  
Describes NVIDIA's G80/CUDA architecture, the moment the GPU stops being purely graphics hardware and becomes a massively parallel general-purpose processor.

**[micikevicius2017]** Micikevicius, P. et al. (2017). *Mixed Precision Training*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03740  
Shows FP16 training on Tensor Cores roughly halves memory and compute cost with no accuracy loss, the software-side unlock that made Tensor Core hardware actually usable at scale.

**[ouyang2022]** Ouyang, L. et al. (2022). *Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback* (InstructGPT). https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155  
Introduces the RLHF alignment technique that turned raw GPT-3 into a usable assistant, the direct technical precursor to ChatGPT.

**[raina2009]** Raina, R., Madhavan, A. and Ng, A. Y. (2009). *Large-Scale Deep Unsupervised Learning Using Graphics Processors*. https://doi.org/10.1145/1553374.1553486  
First clear demonstration that GPUs accelerate deep learning by an order of magnitude over CPUs, a direct precursor to AlexNet.
